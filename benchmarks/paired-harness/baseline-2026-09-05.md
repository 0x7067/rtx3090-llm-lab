# Baseline: production-v18

Single-arm observations; no quality-equivalence or speedup verdict.

Measured 2026-09-05 on the existing RTX 3090 `llama:cuda-swap-v18` service,
Qwen3.8-27B UD-Q4_K_XL-v3 with Q4_K_M DFlash2 plus ngram speculation,
q4_0 KV and two slots sharing a 131,072-token pool. Requests used temperature
0, top_p 1, medium reasoning, seed 42, and up to 4,096 output tokens for
quality. This is the authored synthetic **development/regression** suite from
`build_workload.py`, not independently sampled production tasks. Coding scores
mean the generated code passed the authored assertions, not formal verification
of every requirement.

All 58 requests completed: 54 quality cases passed and four performance probes
finished. Their summed client wall time was 668.04 seconds. The two deepest
retrieval prompts were 114,813 and 114,806 tokens, taking 147.45 and 147.50
seconds respectively. Fifteen requests reported nonzero cached-prefix tokens;
the service was shared and its caches were not administratively reset. Do not
interpret these observations as a cold-cache engine comparison.

The harness and workload passed 17 automated tests, including HTTP integration,
Docker execution, gold-reference checks, and rejection of five plausible
boundary regressions. Full requests, responses, runtime metadata, and timing
events remain in the local, gitignored `runs/workload-v1/` directory. The
machine-readable summary is [baseline-2026-09-05.json](baseline-2026-09-05.json).
